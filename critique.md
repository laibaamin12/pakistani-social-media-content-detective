# AI Plan Critique

## Context
I analysed a simulated dataset of 3,000 Pakistani social media posts. Using my
findings, I asked an AI to write a 2-week posting plan for a Lahore clothing brand.
Below is my check of that plan against my data.

**Note: the dataset is simulated, so results show the method, not real audience behaviour.**

## What matched my data
The AI plan matched my findings for platform (TikTok), content type (Short Video/Reel),
topic (Cricket & PSL), day (Friday), audience (Gen Z) and city (Lahore). It also
labelled its own assumptions honestly.

## Weaknesses I found
1. **Topic fit checked and supported.** I filtered to Fashion & Lifestyle posts
   (431 posts). Cricket & PSL was still the top topic (median 48.3 per 1,000 followers),
   ahead of Ramadan & Eid (37.6) and Fashion & Weddings (28.8). Topic samples are small
   (28 to 64 posts each).
2. **Reel vs Live Stream.** The AI recommended only Reels (42.4), but Live Stream was
   almost as strong (40.3). A better plan would test both.
3. **Runner-up topic ignored.** Ramadan & Eid (37.6) is a strong second topic and fits
   clothing (Eid outfits).
4. **Other days were ranked in my data.** Friday (34.4), Sunday (30.0), Saturday (29.3),
   Thursday (28.7). Monday to Wednesday were weakest (about 25). The AI was not given
   these tables.
5. **20:00 is not uniquely best.** Hours 20, 22, 23 and 21 were nearly tied
   (38.8, 38.6, 38.2, 38.1). A window of 8pm to midnight is a better recommendation.
6. **Posting frequency was an assumption**, not data-driven.

## Revised recommendation
Post short videos on TikTok on Friday between 8pm and midnight, with Sunday and
Saturday as backup days. Focus on Cricket & PSL themes, with Ramadan & Eid as the
second theme. Test one Live Stream per week alongside Reels. Use Facebook as a
secondary platform (28.0). Keep the tone positive, target Gen Z in Lahore, and use
mixed Urdu-English.
